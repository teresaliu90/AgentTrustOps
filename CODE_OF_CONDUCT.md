# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Be constructive, assume good intent while examining technical claims rigorously, protect private
and customer data, and focus criticism on behavior and code rather than people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of secrets or vulnerabilities are not
acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their
GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must follow `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and users.

This concise policy may be replaced by a community-standard code of conduct as the contributor
base grows.
